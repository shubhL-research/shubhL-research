### Research

- 📄 **[Learning Dynamics in Algorithmic Bertrand Competition](https://github.com/shubhL-research/computational-economics-research/tree/main/algorithmic-bertrand-competition)** — Q-learning agents in Bertrand competition produce three convergent regimes (competitive, collusive, chaotic) with path-dependent basins. Extends [Calvano et al. (2020) AER](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.20190623) from differentiated to homogeneous goods.

- 📄 **[Entropy as Macroscopic Order Parameter in Financial Crisis Dynamics](https://github.com/shubhL-research/computational-economics-research/tree/main/financial-crisis-entropy)** — Shannon entropy on BIS credit-growth distributions detects pre-crisis phase transitions across 12 advanced economies (1952–2023).
